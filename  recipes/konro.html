Konro

Sop konro is a signature dish from Makassar, South Sulawesi,
featuring an abundance of meat and highly authentic spices.

Ingredients

  • 2 kg beef ribs
  • Red onion
  • garlic
  • Ginger
  • nutmeg
  • Cumin
  • Kluwek
  • Coriander
  • Brown sugar
  • Roasted candlenuts
  • lemongrass
  • Bay leaf
  • Lime leaves
  • Beef bouillon powder
  • Scallions (garnish)
  • Fried shallots (garnish)

Directions

Step 1

Boil the beef bones twice, replacing the water each time.

Step 2

Grind the spices—such as shallots, garlic, candlenuts, ginger, cumin,
coriander, and kluwek—into a paste, then sauté.

Step 3

Sauté the ground spice paste along with the bruised
lemongrass, bay leaves, lime leaves, and palm sugar.

Step 4

Add the sautéed spice paste to the pot of simmering meat.

Step 5

Add the stock, salt, and seasoning/flavor enhancer.

Step 6

Cook until fully cooked through.

Step 7

Serve garnished with scallions and fried shallots.
