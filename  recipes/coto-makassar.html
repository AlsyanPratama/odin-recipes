Coto Makassar

This is Coto Makassar, an authentic dish from Makassar, South Sulawesi.
Coto Makassar features tender pieces of meat in a broth rich with an abundance of spices.

Ingredients

  • 730 g beef trimmings + offal
  • 90 g roasted peanuts
  • 2 L rice water (the third rinse of the rice)
  • 3 lime leaves
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 piece of galangal, bruised
  • 2 stalks of lemongrass, bruised
  • Oil, as needed, for sautéing

Ground spice paste

  • 1 tbsp peppercorns
  • 1 tbsp coriander seeds
  • 7 shallots
  • 5 cloves of garlic
  • 5 candlenuts
  • 1/2 nutmeg
  • Salt to taste

Complementary ingredients

  • 3 stems of spring leaves / green onions
  • Sufficient celery leaves / soup leaves
  • Fried shallots, to taste
  • Rice / rice cake (ketupat)
  • Chilli sauce
  • A slice of lime

Directions

Step 1

First, thoroughly wash the beef trimmings and offal. Boil them until nearly cooked (about 30 minutes) in rice-rinsing water,
along with galangal, lemongrass, bay leaves, and lime leaves.

Step 2

Roast the peanuts.

Step 3

Prepare and grind the spice paste. Mix it with the peanuts (peeled, if desired).

Step 4

Drain the meat trimmings and cut the offal into pieces. As for the broth,
remove any foam or congealed fat that may have formed to ensure it is clean.

Step 5

Sauté the ground spice paste and peanuts. Add the beef trimmings and offal, then pour in the rice-rinsing water. Cook until done.
Adjust the seasoning to taste. Garnish with leeks and celery leaves.

Step 6

Serve with accompaniments. Ready to eat.
